## Brand & Style

This design system frames public governance and civic intelligence not as an imposing administrative ledger or sterile control terminal, but as an open, welcoming town square. It bridges the gap between municipal administrators and residents through transparency, warmth, and cognitive clarity.

The visual ethos blends **Human-Centered Modernism** with **Soft Nordic Utility**:
- **Warm & Reassuring:** Surfaces avoid cold clinical grays and stark terminal blacks in favor of soft cream, alabaster, and balanced warm-slate backdrops.
- **Airy & Legible:** Generous whitespace, deliberate breathing room, and soft boundaries remove institutional intimidation.
- **Approachable Geometry:** Pronounced rounded edges and smooth contours lend a sense of care and safety to complex public data, budgets, and urban planning feeds.
- **Empathetic Dignity:** Status indicators utilize gentle pastels rather than jarring alarms, guiding the user's attention without inducing panic or administrative fatigue.

## Colors

The color palette centers on trusted civic tones softened by organic, warm undertones. It maintains WCAG AAA compliance for core textual readability while infusing the interface with restorative nature-inspired hues.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#1E3A5F` - Deep Civic Slate):** Imparts dignity, accountability, and clarity. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis headers, and key structural landmarks.
- **Secondary (`#2E7D6B` - Sage Pine):** Evokes community wellbeing, environmental progress, and constructive resolutions. Used for success states, approvals, verified indicators, and constructive actions.
- **Tertiary (`#D97746` - Terracotta Clay):** Brings organic warmth and civic engagement. Used selectively for spotlighted civic initiatives, public hearings, and active participation cues.
- **Neutral (`#666F7B` - Slate Neutral):** Grounded medium slate used to balance backgrounds, muted metadata, and supportive boundary dividers without visual noise.

### Functional Pastels & Semantic Tints
To eliminate administrative stress, status alerts use muted, pastel-backed tokens paired with deeply contrasting text:
- **Gentle Sage (Resolution/Active):** Background `#E7F3EF`, Foreground `#1A4F44`
- **Calm Sky Blue (Informational/Pending Review):** Background `#EAF2FA`, Foreground `#184E77`
- **Warm Amber (Action Required/In Progress):** Background `#FEF3E7`, Foreground `#8C4A11`
- **Soft Rose (Urgent Attention/Delayed):** Background `#FDEEEB`, Foreground `#8F2D24`

### Canvas & Surface Structure
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF9F6` (Alabaster Linen)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Modals):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright Surface)
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Nested Groupings):** `#F4F1EB` (Soft Sandstone)
- **Dividers & Subtle Borders:** `#E8E4DC` (Warm Oat)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered to demystify complex municipal datasets, legal descriptions, and service requests.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines, Titles, and Labels):** With its open counters, humanist curves, and clean geometric baseline, it projects approachability and transparency. It avoids rigid bureaucracy while upholding institutional trustworthiness.
- **Inter (Body and Data Dense Displays):** Renowned for exceptional legibility across digital resolutions, Inter ensures that multi-paragraph civic resolutions, ordinance texts, and tabular metric dashboards remain comfortable to parse over long reading sessions.

### Layout Principles
- Never use all-caps for body text or alerts; title and sentence case maintain a conversational, respectful tone.
- Restrict line lengths for descriptive policy texts to a maximum of 68 characters to prevent reader fatigue.
- Tabular figures (`tnum`) should be activated for monetary sums, municipal budget breakdowns, and timeline indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an adaptive **Fluid Grid** anchored inside comfortable outer constraints. The goal is to avoid sprawling, edge-to-edge data walls that cause mental strain.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px) and `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Cards collapse into single-column vertical stacks.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (1.5rem / 24px) and `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Analytical split screens transition to dual stacked cards.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1360px` with `margin-lg` (3rem / 48px) and `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Generous negative space lines the periphery, keeping dashboards balanced and readable.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence relies on multiples of `0.5rem` (8px). Every analytical widget and summary panel must use `space-lg` (24px) internal padding to maintain an airy, human-centered feel. Related data items (e.g., metric label and value) sit `space-xs` (4px) or `space-sm` (8px) apart, while unrelated civic modules are separated by `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

To replace severe bureaucratic boundaries with a soothing aesthetic, this design system relies on **Ambient Warm Shadows** combined with **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines**. Surfaces float gently above the canvas without creating distracting depth disparities.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FBF9F6`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Content Cards & Neutral Containers):** Border of `1px solid #E8E4DC` paired with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Panels):** Slight upward shift accompanied by a deeper ambient shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.07), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Civic Dialogs):** Centered floating depth: `0 20px 36px -8px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.04)`, layered over an alabaster backdrop overlay with soft blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px); background: rgba(251, 249, 246, 0.8)`).

### Lighting Direction
Simulate a soft, natural overhead skylight. Highlight borders along the top edge of elevated containers are set to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` to produce an organic, tactile bevel.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an intentionally friendly, curvaceous form language that rejects rigid, institutional boxes.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, tooltips, nested mini-badges, and small utility buttons.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Main interactive buttons, status chips, standard dashboard cards, and navigation bars.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Hero feature banners, municipal alert modules, modal dialogs, and segmented container wrappers.
- **Pill Contours (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for category filters, status pill badges, avatar containers, and live civic indicator tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with Primary Deep Slate (`#1E3A5F`), white `label-lg` typography, `rounded-lg` (16px) corners, and generous padding (`12px 24px`). Hover states apply a smooth scale transition and a warm subtle luminance boost.
- **Secondary:** Filled with a soft tone (`#F4F1EB`), text in `#1E3A5F`, and a subtle border (`1px solid #E8E4DC`).
- **Tertiary / Civic Action:** Filled with Sage Pine (`#2E7D6B`) or Terracotta Clay (`#D97746`) for constructive community contributions and proposals.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with `label-sm` or `label-md` weight.
- Never use intense, glaring neon reds or greens. Combine soft pastel tints with deep, accessible label text:
  - *Scheduled / In Discussion:* Calm Sky Blue (`#EAF2FA` / `#184E77`)
  - *Active / Resolved:* Gentle Sage (`#E7F3EF` / `#1A4F44`)
  - *Community Feedback:* Warm Amber (`#FEF3E7` / `#8C4A11`)
  - *Action Pending:* Soft Rose (`#FDEEEB` / `#8F2D24`)
- Include an optional 6px circular dot indicator matching the text tone to assist colorblind accessibility.

### Cards & Civic Intelligence Modules
- Backed with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a soft oat line (`1px solid #E8E4DC`) and `rounded-xl` (24px) geometry.
- Generous internal padding (`24px` to `32px`).
- Header structures feature a clear two-line hierarchy: an uppercase micro-label in `label-sm` for department classification (e.g., "PARKS & INFRASTRUCTURE"), followed by a `headline-sm` title.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls feature a light sandstone background (`#F4F1EB`) with zero idle border to blend harmoniously into the page, transitioning to pure white with a `2px solid #1E3A5F` focus ring upon user interaction.
- Checkboxes and radios use `rounded` (6px) and full-circle shapes respectively, filled with Sage Pine or Deep Slate upon selection.

### Civic Metrics & KPI Modules
- Minimalist presentation: Large numeral values set in `display-lg` using tabular figures, accompanied by a mini trend pill chip and an explanatory sub-caption in `body-sm`.
- Micro charts and sparklines use gentle rounded bar caps and soft line paths without harsh grid lines.
