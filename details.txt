GovGrid: Digital Public Infrastructure & Capital Reconciliation Engine
Project Specification & Feature Dossier for Stitch (Google) UI/UX Upgrade
Executive Overview & Purpose
GovGrid is a Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) platform that bridges the gap between unstructured citizen distress signals (multilingual voice notes, WhatsApp reports, public grievances) and government procurement budgets (GeM e-tenders, municipal capital sanctions).

By executing automated spatial cross-matching (BigQuery GIS ST_DWithin) and multimodal discrepancy audits (Gemini 1.5 Pro on Vertex AI), GovGrid alerts municipal commissioners to:

Unfunded Liabilities: High-distress citizen hotspots where zero budget has been sanctioned.
Capital Leakage / Ghost Projects: 100% disbursed or active civil tenders where citizen distress remains critically high.

Map Tiles	Esri World Dark Gray Canvas tiles (clean, un-watermarked high-contrast GIS base).

Data Models & Contracts for Stitch
Grievance Schema
typescript
interface Complaint {
  id: string; // e.g. "CMP-2026-0891"
  category: "Water Supply" | "Roads & Infrastructure" | "Sewage & Drainage" | "Electricity" | "Solid Waste";
  description: string;
  original_transcript?: string;
  language: string; // "Telugu", "Hindi", "Kannada", etc.
  severity_score: number; // 1 to 10
  lat: number;
  lng: number;
  ward: string;
  source: "WhatsApp Audio" | "Jan-Sunwai Web" | "IVR Toll-Free";
  status: "Open" | "Assigned" | "Under Audit" | "Resolved";
  created_at: string;
}
Tender Schema
typescript
interface Tender {
  id: string; // e.g. "GEM-2025-C-84912"
  title: string;
  department: string;
  budget_inr: number;
  disbursed_inr: number;
  contractor: string;
  completion_pct: number;
  target_lat: number;
  target_lng: number;
  radius_meters: number;
  status: "Active" | "Completed" | "Under Review";
  flagged_leakage: boolean;
}
